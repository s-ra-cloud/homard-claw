---
name: HomardClaw art direction
description: Durable visual direction for the office world and lobster agents.
---

HomardClaw should read as a colorful, richly detailed 16-bit office world with recognizable lobster employees. Avoid sparse early-console pixel grids, heavy terminal framing, or abstract lobsters.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the initial 8-bit/terminal treatment because the lobsters were hard to recognize and asked for an SNES-era feel similar in warmth and density to a colorful game-development office.

**How to apply:** Give sprites clear shell anatomy, claws, antennae, faces, clothing, and readable poses. Use layered shading, furniture, tiled floors, environmental detail, and warm color while keeping controls legible.
