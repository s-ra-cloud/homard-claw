---
name: HomardClaw art direction
description: Durable visual direction for the office world and lobster agents.
---

HomardClaw should read as a dense, cozy isometric pixel-art office diorama with a recognizable lobster employee as the focal point. Avoid sparse early-console grids, heavy terminal framing, abstract lobsters, and straight-on rows of identical desks.

**Why:** The owner rejected both the initial 8-bit terminal treatment and the later front-facing office row. Their preferred reference is an intimate isometric cutaway room with a large lobster working at a richly furnished desk.

**How to apply:** Give the lobster clear shell anatomy, eye stalks, antennae, claws, legs, and working poses. Use cutaway walls, dimensional tile, warm daylight, plants, shelving, desk clutter, and subdued controls around the room.

# The agent avatars are the office lobster, not a lookalike

Every agent avatar must be the same character that appears in the office diorama —
same silhouette, same shading, same palette family. Shell pigment is the only thing
that varies between agents.

**Why:** a hand-drawn stand-in was rejected outright ("I want the exact same lobsters
as in the marlow office scene"), even though it carried the right features. Close-in-
spirit is not close enough here; the owner compares avatars against the scene art
directly.

**How to apply:** derive avatars from the scene artwork rather than redrawing them, and
keep one canonical sprite as the single source of truth for the character.
